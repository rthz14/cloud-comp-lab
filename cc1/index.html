About the Institution

Established in 2007 under the aegis of the Bhandary Foundation, Sahyadri College of Engineering and Management (SCEM) is a premier technological institution nestled along the Mangalore-Bangalore National Highway 48. The campus boasts state-of-the-art infrastructure combined with an ecosystem built directly for innovation, incubation, and research excellence.

NAAC Accredited Grade 'A' NBA Approved Programs AICTE Recognized

Institutional Goals

Our Vision

To be a premier institution in Technology and Management by fostering excellence in education, innovation, incubation and values to inspire and empower the young minds.

Our Mission

  • M1: Creating an academic ambience to impart holistic education focusing on individual growth, integrity, ethical values and social responsibility.
  • M2: Develop skill based learning through industry-institution interaction to enhance competency and promote entrepreneurship.
  • M3: Fostering innovation and creativity through competitive environment with state-of-the-art infrastructure.

Computer Science & Engineering

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering was established in 2007 to empower future computational professionals with a rigorous foundation and technical adaptability. It addresses modern software engineering fields alongside specialized minor verticals like Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Cyber Security.

Departmental Goals

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center for imparting quality technical education through innovative research and incubation with moral values in the field of computer science and engineering

Our Mission

  • M1: Fostering effective education through internal collaboration with industries to address societal demands
  • M2: Empowering professionals with strong ethical values and leadership abilities
  • M3: Developing research and entrepreneurship culture in students and faculty to cultivate lifelong learning
